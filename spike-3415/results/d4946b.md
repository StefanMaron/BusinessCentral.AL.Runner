DATAS with `GCConserveMemory=9` missed the bar. On the corpus, CPU went up 33–40%, and that cost comes from the conserve-memory setting, so that combination won't be the default.

The owner chose to **measure DATAS on its own** next:

- **Runs:** 5 per cell, cold and warm, on the corpus, with nothing else running on the machine.
- **Bar:** the same as before. Cold wall time up by no more than 10%, warm wall time up by no more than 2%, and the same pass/fail set.
- **If it passes:** ship DATAS alone as the default, with the standard `DOTNET_GCDynamicAdaptationMode=0` opt-out.
- **If it fails:** keep Server GC as it is.
